# How do I approach developing programs?
**What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?**
I learned how to transform objects by adjusting their dimensions, positions, and rotation in a 3D environment. I used this to improve my scene from nothing but planes on in a black environment to what it is now. I also used the strategy of keeping similar objects and simply adjusting their values to keep things simple.

**How did iteration factor into your development?**
The iteration factor played a major role in how I improved my project throughout the semester. When I first began the project, I initially put 3 planes down for my table and two mousepads, then created my Porygon2 figure. The Porygon2 figure was much to massive and way too in the way for the entire project, but as I learned more about OpenGL and the 3D environment I was able to find a way to adjust the dimentions of the Porygon2 however I liked along with rotating it with all the parts moving in compliance.

**How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?**
My approach to developing the code throughout the milestones began with simply just completing the milestones, to adding whatever I wanted as long as it met the requirements. For example, I wanted to add lights coming from the monitor and thus added those lights to fit the scene.
